Two takes (eat-1.mp3, eat-2.mp3) are encoded at 128 kbps mono from the
xechoz/snake WAV sources. They are registered as the dedicated `diningEat` key:
only table meals use them, while harvesting and feeding keep the platform synth
`eat` cue. The platform audio manager picks one take at random for each bite, so
repeated meals do not sound identical.
