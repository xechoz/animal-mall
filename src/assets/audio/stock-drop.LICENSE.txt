Stock drop — Animal Mall original asset

Generated procedurally for this project with Python's standard library
(`wave`, `math`, `random`) on 2026-11-04, then encoded with ffmpeg.

Two takes (`stock-drop-1.mp3`, `stock-drop-2.mp3`) of a soft wicker/wooden
"tok": a band-passed noise tick, a short damped wooden body with a falling
pitch, and a quiet low thump. The audio manager picks one take at random for
each landing, so repeated stocking does not sound identical.
